5. **Kernels beyond SVM.** Explain the RBF kernel as a similarity function with one clear illustration or short runnable example. Show the accurate connections to earlier models in a compact table:
   - Ridge has a direct exact kernel version: `KernelRidge(kernel="rbf")`. Students compare it with ordinary `Ridge` on the same train/validation rows, saving `kernel_ridge_result` and `ridge_result` without overwriting earlier variables.
   - Lasso and logistic regression can follow an **approximate explicit RBF feature map** such as `RBFSampler` in a pipeline. Provide brief runnable examples or a small optional student challenge; say that Lasso's sparsity then applies to transformed features, not necessarily original brain regions.    - Standard decision trees/random forests have no kernel switch and already model nonlinear relations through splits. A transformed feature map can be supplied as input, but it is not a kernelized tree. Explain this conceptually; do not add a costly or misleading tree experiment.
