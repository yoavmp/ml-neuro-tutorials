# References

A bibliography for works actually invoked by the published notebooks and
syllabus. For a practical link directory (datasets, documentation, and other
non-bibliographic resources), see [Resources](resources.md).

1. Gareth James, Daniela Witten, Trevor Hastie, Robert Tibshirani, and
   Jonathan Taylor (2023). *[An Introduction to Statistical Learning: with
   Applications in Python](https://hastie.su.domains/ISLP/ISLP_website.pdf.download.html)*.
   Springer. This course's own text, used throughout Exercises 1-9; see
   also the [Syllabus](syllabus.md).

2. Adriana Di Martino, David O'Connor, Bosi Chen, et al. (2017). "Enhancing
   studies of the connectome in autism using the autism brain imaging data
   exchange II." *Scientific Data*, 4, 170010.
   [DOI: 10.1038/sdata.2017.10](https://doi.org/10.1038/sdata.2017.10).
   The original publication for the ABIDE II dataset Exercises 1-9 are
   built on; see [Resources](resources.md) for the dataset's own official
   site and the course's curated data exports.

3. R. A. I. Bethlehem, J. Seidlitz, S. R. White, et al. (2022). "Brain
   charts for the human lifespan." *Nature*, 604, 525-533.
   [DOI: 10.1038/s41586-022-04554-y](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-022-04554-y).
   Cited in Exercise 5 (Regularization and feature selection).

4. A. B. Storsve, A. M. Fjell, C. K. Tamnes, L. T. Westlye, K. Overbye,
   H. W. Aasland, and K. B. Walhovd (2014). "Differential longitudinal
   changes in cortical thickness, surface area and volume across the adult
   life span: regions of accelerating and decelerating change." *Journal of
   Neuroscience*, 34(25), 8488-8498.
   [DOI: 10.1523/JNEUROSCI.0391-14.2014](https://doi.org/10.1523/JNEUROSCI.0391-14.2014).
   Cited in Exercise 5 (Regularization and feature selection).
